# satuuu99 Website Content, Imagery & SEO Handover

**Prepared:** 18 September 2026  
**Audience:** Brand designer, photographer, content designer, developer and SEO partner  
**Primary market:** Ahrensburg, Kreis Stormarn and northeast Hamburg  
**Primary conversion:** Select a suitable treatment and book an appointment

## 1. Executive summary

The website currently has **26 indexable URLs**, approximately **4,681 rendered German words**, 10 service detail pages, five journal articles and three new location-focused service pages. The visual system and interaction design are strong. The next production phase should make the website equally strong in evidence, explanation and authentic imagery.

The recommended mature site contains:

- **20,000–28,000 German words** across commercial, local and editorial pages.
- **45–55 final image assets**, created from approximately 120–180 original photographs captured in one or two shoots.
- **One hero film plus 8–12 short silent motion clips** for ambient use.
- **6–10 meaningful sections per important commercial page**.
- **12–18 substantial journal articles** during the first editorial year.
- A real photograph for every treatment family and every commercially important URL.

The goal is not to repeat keywords. Each page must answer a distinct visitor question, provide verifiable business information and lead naturally to a relevant treatment or booking action.

| Foot rituals | 4–6 | Foot bath, towel preparation, care tools, foot massage, clean product still life |
| Brand and still life | 4–6 | Linen, water, stone, ceramic bowl, gift material, treatment preparation |

### Motion assets

- One **12–20 second homepage hero loop**, landscape 16:9, with a stable area for typography.
- 8–12 **three-to-six-second silent detail loops**: water, fabric, hands preparing a room, product texture, Gua Sha movement and arrival details.
- Capture at least one clean vertical version of key moments for mobile and social use.
- Avoid rapid montage, obvious stock gestures, dramatic steam effects or footage that makes the studio appear larger than it is.

### Crop and delivery requirements

For every important subject, deliver:

- Landscape: 2400 × 1600 px minimum.
- Portrait: 1600 × 2200 px minimum.
- Square: 1600 × 1600 px minimum when the composition permits.
- Web exports in AVIF or WebP plus preserved high-resolution masters.
- Neutral colour grade using the brand's earth, stone, sand, ivory and shadow palette.
- No baked-in text or logos.
- File names should describe the real subject, for example `satuuu99-head-spa-warm-water-01.webp`.

### Authenticity rules

- Photograph the actual Ahrensburg studio, team, tools and treatments.
- Do not show a pool, resort, sauna landscape, hotel suite or other facility that is not available.
- Do not stage medical equipment or diagnostic procedures unless they are genuinely part of the documented service.
- Obtain written model releases and staff approval.
- Avoid showing a treatment step that is not included in the current bookable programme.
- Preserve skin texture and the true scale of the room.

## 7. Current competitive position

### What can be claimed now

The site has a strong technical and structural base:

- Dedicated pages for the three most important local service clusters.
- Unique titles and descriptions.
- Crawlable prerendered HTML.
- LocalBusiness and Service structured data.
- Sitemap, robots file and `llms.txt`.
- Consistent address, telephone and opening-hours information.
- Strong internal linking and bilingual visitor experience.

### What cannot be claimed yet

We cannot honestly claim search dominance from the website alone. Dominance requires Google Search Console impressions, average positions, click-through rate, Google Business Profile performance and ideally a tracked local-rank grid around Ahrensburg. None of those datasets is available in this audit.

The current live search landscape shows established Hamburg Head Spa specialists, marketplace pages and high-review competitors. Broad **“Head Spa Hamburg”** is therefore a high-competition target. The specific **Ahrensburg + service** combinations are the most realistic near-term opportunity because they have stronger geographic relevance and a narrower result set.

## 8. Measurement plan

Before calling the strategy successful, establish these baselines:

1. Connect and verify Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://satuuu99.de/sitemap.xml`.
3. Record impressions, clicks, CTR and average position for every target cluster monthly.
4. Track calls, email clicks, directions and outbound booking clicks as conversions.
5. Review Google Business Profile searches, views, calls and direction requests.
6. Run a local grid for the five Tier 1 phrases from points around Ahrensburg every four weeks.
7. Review queries that reach positions 8–20 and expand the matching page with useful missing information.

## 10. Content approval checklist

Before publishing new copy or imagery, confirm:

- The treatment is currently offered and bookable.
- Every described step occurs in the real treatment.
- Duration and price link to the current official source.
- No medical outcome is promised.
- Staff qualifications are approved by the named team member.
- The photograph shows the actual studio, team or treatment.
- Model and usage rights are documented.
- German business facts match the Google Business Profile exactly.
- The page answers a distinct visitor need and does not duplicate another page.
